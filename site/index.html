How these numbers are defined

Scope: Zoho Projects built from the Bundle: New Client Profile Project template (Standard Layout) that are not Completed or Cancelled. HR Technology, Benefit Technology, Open Enrollment, 401K and Cancellation projects are left out.

Client issues from Cliq come from tracker.json, which is updated separately from the automatic Zoho refresh (as of —).

Pre go-live means the status is Active, In Compliance, Pending Welcome Call, Pending Questionnaire Return or Active - Dual Maintenance. Live, not passed means 1st, 2nd, 3rd or 3+ Payrolls Ran, or Ready to Pass.

At risk: a pre go-live project whose first check is within — days and is under —% complete, or whose first check date passed in the last — days. Projects not updated in over — days are treated as stale and counted only in the cleanup queue.

Critical checks are the eight project checkboxes: Tax Codes Added, Bank Account and Defaults Verified, Mock Payroll Complete, Profiles Assigned, Delivery Setup Complete, Tax Setup Ticket Submitted, Accounting Billing Ticket Submitted and Questionnaire Completed.

Coming from sales: Zoho CRM deals whose stage is Pending Welcome Call. Insurance and 401k deals are not in that stage, so they do not appear.

CRM first check is the Estimated First Check Date on the deal. A deal is matched to a project when every word of the deal name, apart from words like LLC, Inc, PRT or Deal, appears in the project name, so a wrong or missing match is possible.
